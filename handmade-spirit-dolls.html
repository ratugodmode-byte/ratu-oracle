About Spirit Dolls

Spirit dolls appear in many folk, devotional, and magical traditions as figures of memory, prayer, protection, guidance, and personal meaning. On Ratu Oracle they are described as spiritual collectibles and symbolic companions rather than guaranteed supernatural services.
